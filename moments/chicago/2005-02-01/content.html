Chicago: High School, College & Volleyball Beginnings

2005–2015

Transferred to Gordon Tech on the North Side. Graduated. College. Met the person I married. Started volleyball at eighteen. Ten years in the Windy City is not a trip. It is a life.

Gordon Tech and the lake

Gordon Technical High School sat at California Avenue — all-boys Catholic until it wasn't, Rams on the jersey. The decade underneath the pin is how I became American without stopping being from Varna. North Avenue Beach in summer: ninety-something nets, Polish guys claiming courts at dawn, the lake as a cold shower, the skyline from the sand. That is where volleyball stopped being a sport I had heard of and became the thing that would keep moving me.

What the city was playing

Fall Out Boy and Kanye were local weather. In 2008, if you were in Chicago on election night, Grant Park was the air itself. The rest of the decade was apartments, a marriage, college, and the slow realization that the lake in August is a kind of church if you have a net.

I would come back. The pin for this one is the decade that made the coming-back possible.
