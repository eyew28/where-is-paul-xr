Family Reunion & Wedding in Chicago

October 1–5, 2025

Flew in from Amman via Royal Jordanian after the layover. My step-dad Mike's daughter's wedding, October 3–5. And an emotional reunion with my dad after sixteen years apart — we had reconnected over the phone; he was excited to meet my brother and me in person. Stayed until the 5th, then Miami.

The decade, inverted

This is not the Chicago decade. This is five days that the decade made possible. A wedding. A father. Sixteen years in the gap. The city was just the room it happened in.
