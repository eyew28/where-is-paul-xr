Jazz and Beignets in New Orleans

April 28 – May 1, 2025

Three days. Jazz. Beignets. The French Quarter doing the thing it has been doing since before any of us arrived.

Quarter hours

You hear the music before you decide to look for it. Powdered sugar on everything. April in New Orleans is already warm enough. Three days is a taste, which is all a first visit should be if you want to come back.
