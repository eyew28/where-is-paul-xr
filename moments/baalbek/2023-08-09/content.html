Ancient Ruins in Baalbek

August 9, 2023

A day in Baalbek standing inside the Temple of Bacchus. That is the kind of ruin that makes you understand why people used to think gods needed architecture.

The stones

Baalbek is in the Bekaa. The Temple of Bacchus is better preserved than it has any right to be — columns the size of a decision. One day is not enough and also exactly enough: you go, you stand there, you leave smaller.
