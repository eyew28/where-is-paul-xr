Art and Architecture in Barcelona

August 21 – September 12, 2023

Twenty-three days. Gaudí. La Rambla. The long stay — the one that should have been a post and wasn't.

A city you stay in

Barcelona in late August is still summer and already thinking about not being summer. Sagrada Família is the building that refuses to be finished, which is the correct personality for this city. Park Güell, the grids, the rambla at the hour when it is more locals than suitcases. Twenty-three days is long enough to have a coffee place and short enough to leave before it becomes a life.
