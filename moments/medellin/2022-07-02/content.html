Exploring Medellín's Markets

July 2022

After the decade, the restart. A month of wandering Medellín's markets — Homecenter and beyond — soaking in a city that used to be a warning and had become a lesson in how a place can change its name in the world's mouth.

Markets, not museums

I was not here for a transformation tour. I was here to walk, eat, and be in a valley that stays spring. The markets are where Medellín tells you what it actually is: fruit stacked like argument, motorcycles, the particular humidity of 1,500 meters.

Medellín in 2022 still carried that post-pandemic hunger to be outside. Reggaeton from the storefronts. The metrocable over the hills. Comuna 13's murals on a neighborhood that once had invisible borders you could die for crossing. City of eternal spring is a slogan until 6 p.m. when the brick goes gold.
