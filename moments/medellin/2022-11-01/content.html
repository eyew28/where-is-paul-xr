Vibrant Streets of Medellín

November 2022

Two more months. Comuna 13's street art. Salsa nights. The colorful streets people put on postcards, which are also real streets with real hills and escalators climbing a neighborhood that once had invisible borders.

Comuna 13

The outdoor escalators, the murals, hip-hop as the thing that replaced the sound of the war — I walked it. Tourism had already arrived. The art was still the point. Salsa at night is not a performance if you are actually on the floor.

Medellín in 2022 still carried that post-pandemic hunger to be outside. Reggaeton from the storefronts. The metrocable over the hills. Comuna 13's murals on a neighborhood that once had invisible borders you could die for crossing. City of eternal spring is a slogan until 6 p.m. when the brick goes gold.
