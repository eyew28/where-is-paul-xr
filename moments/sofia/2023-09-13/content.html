Hidden Gems in Sofia

September 13 – October 2023

Thirty-eight days back in the capital I used to treat as a playground. Churches. Markets. Sofia as a visitor in a city that still knows my feet.

The same streets, different speed

Alexander Nevsky is still the gold dome you navigate by. Vitosha Boulevard is still downtown. The metro exists now. The mausoleum does not. I was here for churches and markets and the particular feeling of being from a place without living in it. Thirty-eight days is a visit that starts to pretend it is a season.
