Island Life in Honolulu

September 2024 · 38 days

Island life. Beaches. Lush hikes. Honolulu as a real stay, not a layover with pineapples.

Waikiki is the front door

You land in the postcard and then you find the trails. Diamond Head sitting on the end of the island like a period. Thirty-eight days is long enough to get tired of Waikiki and go looking for the rest of Oahu, which is the point.
