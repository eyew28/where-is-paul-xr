Luxury and Adventure in Dubai

October 5–8, 2023

Four days. Skyline. Desert dunes. A layover that dressed up as a trip.

Vertical, then sand

Dubai is the argument that a city can be invented in one generation and still have a creek. Burj Khalifa does the obvious thing. The desert outside the ring road does the older thing. I did both, which is the correct four-day itinerary whether you admit it or not.
