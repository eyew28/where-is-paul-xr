Sun and Art in St. Petersburg

January – August 2023

Two hundred and seventeen days. Sun. Murals in artsy downtown St. Pete. This is the stay that turned Florida from a postcard into a home base.

The long one

St. Petersburg does murals, the pier, a downtown that wants to be a beach town with better coffee. I soaked up the sun and walked the art. Seven months is not a trip. It is the chapter where the crew — the people I keep coming back to — gets an address.
