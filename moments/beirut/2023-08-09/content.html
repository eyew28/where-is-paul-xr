History and Vibes in Beirut

August 2023 · 8 days

Beirut's historic souks. Nightlife along the Mediterranean. A week on a coast that feels like Varna if Varna had layers of civil war and French mandate and a downtown that keeps being rebuilt.

Corniche hours

The Corniche is the city's living room the way the Sea Garden is Varna's — you walk it at night because the sea is the only thing that didn't pick a side. Raouché rocks sitting in the water. Arabic and French in the same sentence. The souks are for getting lost on purpose.

August 2023 in Beirut was hot, loud, and still itself. I did not write it down then. The pin waited.
