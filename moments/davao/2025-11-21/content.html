Arrival in Davao: Birthday Energy & Property Exploration

November 21, 2025

Landed early after the 2 a.m. Scoot from Singapore. Leo and Boy met me. We celebrated my birthday. Then looking at land — Samal Island first, then Mati three hours by car, more secluded, a little paradise, just really far.

Birthday, then property

That is a sentence I would not have believed in Osseo. Davao as a place you might actually stay. Samal sitting in the water as the top choice. They dropped me back in the city and headed home.
