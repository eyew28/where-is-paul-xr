Ancient Wonders in Athens

August 18–20, 2023

Three days. The Acropolis. Greek food in Plaka. The heat coming off the marble.

Up the hill

You walk through Plaka because that is how you approach it — tourist path, yes, and also the only path that has been used for this for a very long time. Then the rock, the Parthenon, August light that does not care about your schedule. Down again for tzatziki and the particular exhaustion of having stood in a place that old.
