# Vision

Where is Paul? is the public life layer of Sci-Fi Labs.

A spatial record of a life on a globe. Same URL on phone, desk, and Vision Pro Safari. Clone it. Swap `moments/moments.js`. That is the product.

## Where it sits

1. **OS** — runs as a website today; native on [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/) tomorrow. You own the desktop the globe opens on.
2. **Private layer** — [Knowledge Graph](https://github.com/paulvisciano/knowledge-graph) holds the raw archive (photos, conversations, files) on an air-gapped drive. The globe only publishes what you put in `moments/`.
3. **Sister apps** — a Musical Cube can drop on the map at the place it was made. Neuro Graph is the public network view of the same life.

Own the OS. Own the apps. Own the data. The globe is how other people walk through the part you chose to share.
